# Expense Claims

1. Open the portal and select **New Claim**.
2. Enter your cost centre and attach receipts.
3. Submit before the 25th of the month.

## Rates

Domestic day trips are reimbursed at the standard rate for the first eight hours
and at the extended rate beyond that, while overnight domestic trips move to the
residential rate once lodging is involved; international trips follow the same
structure but each band is uplifted by the overseas weighting, except that the
extended rate does not apply on the day of return, on which only a half-band is
claimable unless travel exceeds twelve hours, in which case the full extended
rate resumes.

## Processing

Once submitted, raise a PO and match it against the GRN in SAP so finance can
clear the accrual against the correct WBS element before month-end.

## Approvals

Claims above the FSC threshold need sign-off from the T&E owner, who will check
them against the current DSA schedule.
